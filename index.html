About Us

Our Story

A Decade of Honest, Beautiful Photography.

Based in Dar es Salaam, Ever After Photography has spent the past decade documenting weddings, engagements and family milestones across Tanzania, from beach ceremonies to garden receptions.

We believe a photograph should feel like a memory, not a pose. Our team blends candid documentary storytelling with editorial-quality portraits, so your story reads true for years to come.

Common Questions

Pricing FAQ

How do the installments work?+

Pay a 30% deposit to secure your date, then split the remaining balance into equal monthly installments due before your event.

Do you travel outside Dar es Salaam?+

Yes, we regularly shoot in other regions across Tanzania. Travel fees depend on distance and are quoted at booking.

How far in advance should we book?+

We recommend booking 3 to 6 months ahead, especially during peak wedding season.
